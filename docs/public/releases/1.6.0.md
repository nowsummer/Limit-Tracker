## Open source, and updates on your terms

- The source code is now public in this repository under the MIT License. You can read how the app works and build it yourself.
- Gemini automatic tracking is removed. Google's Gemini CLI terms do not allow third-party apps to use the Gemini CLI sign-in, so Gemini is now manual entry only.
- Updates still download in the background, but they install only when you choose to: Restart now to apply in Settings, or Install and restart in the tray menu. Nothing installs at startup, at exit, or on a timer.
- The app now explains the risk of automatic Claude tracking: it uses your Claude Code sign-in in a way Anthropic's policy does not permit for third-party apps, so your Claude account could be restricted. Manual entry avoids this.
- Copies on 1.5.x update themselves once more, to this version or later. From 1.6.0 on, every update waits for your click.
