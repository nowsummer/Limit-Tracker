## Close button, accounts without a 5-hour limit

- The tray popup has a close (×) button next to refresh, so it always closes in one click.
- Accounts without a 5-hour limit now lead with their weekly limit in the tray number, the cards and the iPhone widget, with the next weekly limit beside it instead of an empty "Weekly limit" slot.
- A model limit sent first by the service no longer becomes the tray number of such an account.
- A rounder refresh icon.
- Fixes: the main window no longer grows by a pixel each time a sheet opens at 125% or 150% display scaling, the English "Window" column header no longer wraps, and a message shown just before a language change no longer stays in the old language.
